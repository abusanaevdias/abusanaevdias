## Suggested study sequence

This is a proposed progression of bounded exercises, not completed work or a benchmark of project quality. Choose one repository and one acceptance check at a time.

1. **A small deterministic workflow:** start with InterviewOps' mock provider (#12) or a synthetic resume JSON round trip in Reactive Resume (#13). Separate generated suggestions from verified output.
2. **One operational record:** inspect a Typebot form (#16), Frappe CRM transition (#19) or Chatwoot conversation state (#20). Limit the exercise to one input, one state change and one export.
3. **One measurement path:** use synthetic events or responses to inspect Umami (#18) and Formbricks (#17). Check deduplication, filters and counts before interpreting a report.
4. **One import and reconciliation:** study Actual Budget (#14), then Sure (#15), using synthetic records without bank connections.
5. **One source-grounded learning task:** move from Open Notebook (#3) and Vane (#5) to OpenTutor (#6), DeepTutor (#2), LearnHouse (#7) and OpenMAIC (#1). Keep a fixed source and a teacher-reviewable expected result.
6. **Multiple services and generated artifacts:** study one Presenton export (#4), Meetily transcript (#11) or MoneyPrinterTurbo output manifest (#9), then examine Scira's tool orchestration (#8) and Open Lovable's sandbox edits (#10). Record provider, compute, asset-rights and failure boundaries before expanding scope.
